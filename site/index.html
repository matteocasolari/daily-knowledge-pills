MY BOOKS, MY NOTES

A place to return to.

I’ve kept notes from years of reading. This is a small way to revisit them: five concepts from five of my books each day, mostly nonfiction, with the occasional idea from a novel.

Each reminder pairs an excerpt from my notes with a short refresher. The daily set stays at or below 550 words. The extra links are there for days when I feel like following a thought further.

My notes mix copied passages and personal summaries. “From my notes” keeps the wording I saved, including typos. The short refresher is added context, separate from the excerpt; the excerpt has not been checked against a published edition.

My saved ideas and reading progress stay in this browser. They don’t sync between devices.
